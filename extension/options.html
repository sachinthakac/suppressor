How it decides

  1. Keywords blur an item instantly and always count as the topic. Hints blur instantly too, but the classifier must agree. Use /regex/ for patterns.
  2. Everything else is scored by the classifier: is this about the topic? and which of your reasons fits it best?
  3. An on-topic item is hidden when its hide reasons add up past the threshold. Show reasons give the classifier something else to choose, so news or music isn't forced into a spoiler bucket. With no hide reasons, everything on-topic is hidden.
